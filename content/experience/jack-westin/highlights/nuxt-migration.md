Planned and ran the migration from Nuxt 2 to Nuxt 4 section by section, with both apps live side by side and no downtime. Reworked data fetching, code splitting, and image loading for SSR on MCAT Books (Vue 3, Composition API, TypeScript, Vite): Lighthouse performance went from 36 to 97, LCP from 8.5s to 1.0s, and Total Blocking Time from 1,570ms to 30ms, with accessibility up from 91 to 100 and SEO at 100.
