Designed, built, and shipped Customer Portfolio Management (CPM), a real-time crypto portfolio tracker (Nuxt 4, Bun, TypeScript), as the sole developer, from architecture and data model to deployment. Asset managers register client wallet addresses and exchange API keys, and the app tracks assets, positions, and orders across 16 blockchains and 12 exchanges, with live analytics, charts, and configurable alerts for advisor recommendations.
