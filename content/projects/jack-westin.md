Jack Westin is a US MCAT-prep platform with more than 500,000 student accounts. I worked on its frontend for more than four years and led the frontend team for most of that time.

I built the spaced-repetition flashcards. On the practice exams, the MCAT books and the Chrome extension I led the work and took the heavy tasks. I also architected the AI chat tutor, which streams answers into the Nuxt interface, and the shared design system that four apps use, with Storybook and more than 95% test coverage.

Most of the products are free to try at jackwestin.com.
